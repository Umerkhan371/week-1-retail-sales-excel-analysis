# 📊 Retail Sales Data Performance & Insights Analysis

## 🔹 Project Title

**Retail Transactions Analytics**

## 🔹 Project Objective

The primary objective of this project is to clean, audit, and analyze a raw retail sales dataset containing 1,000 transactions to extract meaningful business insights. By addressing formatting anomalies and structuring the data logically, the project aims to evaluate key performance indicators (KPIs)—such as high-revenue product categories, customer purchasing patterns across demographics (age and gender), and sales volume dynamics—to support data-driven retail strategies.

## 🔹 Tools Used

* **Data Processing & Analytics Platform:** Microsoft Excel (Advanced formulas, descriptive statistics, and dataset management tools).
* **Core Functions Applied:** `SUM`, `AVERAGE`, `MIN`, `MAX`, `SUMIF`, `COUNTIF`, and advanced conditional sorting/filtering.
* **Visualization:** Custom interactive Excel Charts and trend graphs.

## 🔹 Dataset Columns

The core analysis relies on the structured transactional schema found within **Completed Excel File.xlsx**. The specific attributes include:

* `Transaction ID`: A unique identification string for each individual sale.
* `Date`: The chronological timestamp of the purchase record (`YYYY-MM-DD`).
* `Customer ID`: Unique tracking identifier assigned to individual shoppers.
* `Gender`: Demographic classification of the customer (Male / Female).
* `Age`: Numeric age distribution of the customer base.
* `Product Category`: The retail segment of the items sold (Beauty, Clothing, Electronics).
* `Quantity`: The precise count of individual units purchased per transaction.
* `Price per Unit`: The individual retail cost assigned to a single item unit.
* `Total Amount`: The gross monetary transaction value, calculated dynamically as $\text{Quantity} \times \text{Price per Unit}$.

6. **Executive Dashboarding (`Charts` & `Observation`):** Converted aggregated metrics into distinct visual graphs paired with a formal narrative breakdown of retail performance.

## 🔹 Key Observations

*(Extracted directly from the analytical computations and findings documented in the **Observation** and **Basic Analysis** sheets)*

* **Revenue Benchmarks:** The retail operations recorded a comprehensive **Total Sales Amount of 456,000** spanning exactly **1,000 transactions**, maintaining an average order value of **$456**.
* **Top Revenue Generator:** The **Electronics** sector emerged as the highest-grossing category, driving a total sales revenue of **156,905**.
* **Volume Distribution:** A gross volume of **2,514 units** were cleared across the floor. While Electronics led in total revenue, **Clothing** commanded the highest operational movement with **894 total units sold**.
* **Peak Transaction Value:** The maximum single-transaction ceiling hit an impressive **2,000**, contrasted against a baseline entry point minimum sale of **25**.
* **Demographic Footprint:** The consumer baseline reflects an incredibly mature demographic spread, maintaining an **Average Customer Age of 41.3 years**, capturing a broad purchasing audience starting from the youngest tracked consumer at **18 years old** up to senior shoppers at **64 years old**.
